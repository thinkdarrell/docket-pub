Where these dates come from

Meetings come from the council's video site and the city's calendar. A meeting either of them calls cancelled is shown marked cancelled, with the one that says so named. A calendar event docket.pub does not read as a council or committee meeting is left out. A committee meeting is listed once the city puts it on its calendar, which can be only a few days before it is held.

A hearing's date is read by docket.pub from the text of a resolution on a council agenda, without a language model. An agenda is posted before its meeting, so a listed resolution may not yet have been adopted, and a hearing the council later moved may still show on its first date.

The list of hearings can be incomplete. A resolution whose hearing date docket.pub could not read from the agenda's text is left out, and so are hearings held by city boards and commissions other than the council.

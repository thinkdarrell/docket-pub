GUIDANCE — WHAT THIS FILE OWNS
  The boundary of the project: why it exists, what counts as success, what
  is explicitly out of scope, what constraints hold regardless of
  convenience, and what is still unknown. It is the document an agent reads
  to decide whether a proposed change belongs in this project at all, and
  the grading anchor for retrospectives and doc gardening.

GUIDANCE — WHAT IT MUST NOT ABSORB
  How anything is built (`ARCHITECTURE.md`), why a specific technical choice
  was made (`docs/decisions/`), or what work is planned (`plans/`). Goals
  describe the destination, never the route.

GUIDANCE — ANTI-PATTERN THIS FILE EXISTS TO PREVENT
  Scope creep by silence. Without a written non-goals list, every plausible
  adjacent feature looks in scope to an agent that has no way to check, and
  the same scope argument gets re-litigated in chat where its outcome cannot
  be read back later. A non-goal with a one-line reason kills that argument
  permanently.
-->

# The city page

What a reader sees at `/al/<slug>/` (the city page; `city_overview` in `src/docket/web/public.py`, rendering `src/docket/web/templates/city.html`), what the Birmingham page adds under its briefing flag, which rules decide every sentence on it, and what the worker computes for it. Present tense. The page's design and the review that produced it are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-birmingham-city-page-design.md`; this file describes what runs.

## The page, first to last

Every city page opens with the lead (`partials/city_lead.html`: the eyebrow of council type, county, and population from the municipality's metadata, the `<h1>` "<City>, <ST>", and the freshness chip linking to source health), then, on a flagged city only, the opener, then one search field scoped to the city (`partials/briefing/search.html`: a `GET /search` form with a hidden `city`, the label "Search <City>'s agendas, minutes, and transcripts for a street, a company, or a topic.", and a submit button), then the doors in this order, each a `<section>` with one `<h2>` that renders only when its data exists: "Where the money went", "Where they disagreed", "The council", "What they keep dealing with", "Said at the meeting", "Signals in the last 30 days"; then "Recent meetings"; then "From the blog" when the city has published posts. The site-wide source panel and footer follow (below). The partials live under `src/docket/web/templates/partials/briefing/` (`opener.html`, `search.html`, `door_money.html`, `door_disagreed.html`, `door_topics.html`, `door_said.html`, `signals.html`, `meetings.html`); the council cards are `partials/council_card.html`, shared with `/al/<slug>/council/`. Every CSS class the page introduces is prefixed `brf-` (`layout.css`, phone rules in `mobile.css`), and the district line on a council card is `cc-district-num`.

The page is cached per slug for five minutes in the process (`_overview_cache` in `public.py`); the route runs the per-request queries below and reads the precomputed facts in one query. Nothing on the page is a count without a destination: a section with nothing to show is absent, never a zero, and a "More →" link appears only where the page it opens has rows.

The gate is `municipalities.metadata.briefing`: `briefing.is_briefing_city` is true when the JSONB carries `"briefing": true`, which migration `037_city_briefing_facts` sets for Birmingham alone. The opener, the transcript door, and the worker task run only for flagged cities; every other section renders for any city whose rows support it, so `/al/mobile/`, `/al/homewood/`, and `/al/vestavia_hills/` show the lead, the search field, the council, and whatever lists their data fills, with no generated prose. Turning a city on is a metadata change.

## The opener

`briefing.opener_facts(municipality)` (`src/docket/services/briefing.py`) counts the rows and `briefing.opener_beats` writes them as two `<p class="brf-beat">` of plain text and links; nothing in the opener is written by a language model. The last meeting is the newest non-hidden meeting of type `council` that is not upcoming (the predicate in `src/docket/services/meeting_time.py`: date plus start time, or noon, plus three hours, America/Chicago), is not cancelled (its title matches `query.CANCELLED_PREDICATE_SQL`, the regex `cancell?ed`, the only mark Birmingham's rows carry), and has at least one agenda item, so a work session, a planning board, or a placeholder record with no items never becomes "the council met". The first beat reads "Last meeting. The council met on <Weekday, Month D> and acted on <n> items." ("last met on" when the meeting is more than 14 days old; the year is appended when it is not this year), then, when an item passes the dollar check, "The largest was a <$ amount> <headline>." (an item whose `processing_status` is `withdrawn` is never the largest) with the amount and headline together one link to the item page, then one vote sentence, then the action link "Read the <Month D> meeting →" to the meeting page (to the first split vote's anchor when one exists and no other link in the beat already carries it). The vote sentence is one of: "One vote split: <item clause> passed <y> to <n>, with <Member> voting no." (or "abstaining"; the member linked to their record when exactly one dissenter is resolved, otherwise "with one member voting no" or "with two members voting no" linked to the vote's anchor on the meeting page, and with no roll call recorded the sentence ends at the tally); "<Three> votes split." as plain text; "Every vote was unanimous." when the meeting has chosen-source votes and none split; or nothing when it has no chosen-source votes. The item clause is the linked item's headline or title when the vote links exactly one item, "a consent-agenda vote covering <n> items" when several and every linked item is on the consent agenda, "a vote covering <n> items" when several otherwise, and "a vote at the <Month D> meeting" when none. A clause the rows cannot fill is dropped, never defaulted. The second beat reads "Next meeting. <Weekday, Month D>, the nearest upcoming council meeting that is not cancelled, with <n> items on the agenda." (the count only when the agenda has items) and "See the agenda →" to that meeting, or, with no upcoming council meeting, "No agenda is posted yet for the next meeting; agendas usually appear a few days before." With no qualifying last meeting the first beat is "docket.pub has not indexed a <City> meeting yet."

Each sentence is at most 25 words: the headline and the item clause are clipped with `briefing.clip_words` to 25 minus the words the rest of the sentence already holds, never fewer than four, with "…" appended when cut. Each beat carries at most three links, no two adjacent, and no two with one target. A clause counted from video votes ends "from the video"; a minutes-sourced clause says nothing. The same segments, action links left out, joined by a space and cut at the end of the last sentence the opener wrote that fits in 300 characters, are the page's `<meta name="description">` (`briefing.opener_text`), present only when the opener renders.

## The dollar check

An amount is written into prose on this page (the opener, "Where the money went", the transcript door, and the `order=amount` search page) only when `briefing.passes_dollar_check(title, description, headline, dollars_amount)` holds: the item's title and description together contain exactly one dollar amount (`extract_dollar_amounts` in `src/docket/analysis/structured_facts.py`, a set) and it equals the stored `dollars_amount`, or the headline holds a figure `h` within `min(p / 2, h / 10)` of the stored amount, `p` the largest power of ten dividing `h` (a headline "$10.1 million" accepts a stored 10,125,000; "$10 million" accepts 9,600,000 and refuses 14,000,000; "$7 million" refuses 39,500,000). An item that fails is passed over for the next largest and still shows its stored amount on the meeting and item pages. The amount in prose is always the stored one, written by `briefing.amount_in_words` ("$10.1 million", "$2 million", "$850,000", "$1.2 billion"), and a headline that opens with a dollar figure loses it so the figure is not printed twice. A date written as `<Month D>` anywhere on the page carries the year when it is not this year's.

## Which votes count

Every count on the page takes one source per meeting (`briefing.chosen_source_cte`, scoped by the caller to the meetings in question): `minutes_text` when the meeting has any vote from the minutes, otherwise `manual`, then `api`, then `video_ocr`; votes with `needs_review` set are excluded everywhere. A split vote is a chosen-source vote with nays or abstentions above zero. Where the minutes and the video disagree the page does not adjudicate; the meeting page and the minutes-versus-video page show the disagreement.

## The doors

"Where the money went" (`briefing.money_items`): the three largest items of the last 90 days in non-hidden meetings of the city that have taken place and were not cancelled (an upcoming or a cancelled meeting's items are not listed), excluding items the council withdrew (`processing_status = 'withdrawn'`), that pass the dollar check, scanning at most forty rows by amount, each line "<$ amount> — <headline> · <Month D>[ · passed on the consent agenda]" linking to the item page, then "More →" to `/search?city=<slug>&order=amount`. Fewer than three shows what there is; none, and the section is absent.

"Where they disagreed" (`briefing.disagreement`, from the precomputed `split_votes` row): up to five split votes of the last 180 days, newest first, each "<item clause linked> · <passed, failed, or tabled, the stored result> <y> to <n>[ to <a> abstaining] · <Month D> · <dissenters>[ · from the video]", the item clause linking to the item page when one item is linked and to the vote's anchor on the meeting page otherwise, each resolved dissenter linked to their record and an unresolved name plain, with "(abstained)" after an abstainer, then "More →" to `/al/<slug>/split_vote/`. With no split in the window but a split ever, the one sentence "Every recorded vote since <date> was unanimous. The last split was <item clause linked> on <date, with the year when it is not this year>, <y> to <n>." With votes in the window and no split ever, "Every recorded vote since <date> was unanimous." alone. With no facts row (the task has not run), no chosen-source vote at all, or no chosen-source vote in the window, the section is absent.

"The council" (`briefing.council_cards` over `query.list_council_members`): the active roster ordered by district number, members without a district first as "At-Large", each card an `<article class="cc">` whose first and largest text is "District <n>", then the name linked to the member record (the link stretched over the card), then, when the precomputed `member_counts` row has the member, "Attended <a> of <r> roll calls in the last 12 months" and "Voted against the outcome <n> times", the second a link to `/al/<slug>/council/<id>/?filter=dissent&since=<date 12 months back>` when `n` is above zero and plain text when it is zero. A member with no chosen-source roll call in the window shows the name alone; a city with no active members has no section. `/al/<slug>/council/` renders the same card without the numbers.

"What they keep dealing with" (`briefing.topics_door`): under "Tracked in <City>", each of the city's policy badges (`query.list_city_policy_badges`) as "<name linked to /al/<slug>/<badge>/> · <n> items since <Month D> · <example headline linked>", the count and example under the gates the category landing uses (`status = 'applied'`, confidence at least 0.6, `processing_status = 'completed'`, the city's significance threshold), the window the last 90 days with no upper bound; a priority with no item in the window shows "last item <Month D, YYYY>" and its newest item ever, and one with no qualifying item ever is left out. Under "Busiest topics", the five topics with the most items in the window, `routine` excluded, each "<name linked to /topics/<slug>/?city=<slug>> · <n> items since <Month D> · <example linked>". The section renders when either list is non-empty.

"Said at the meeting" (`briefing.said_at_the_meeting`, flagged cities only): the newest non-hidden meeting of the city with a public transcript (`transcripts.public_predicate`: `uploaded_at` set and status not `low_speech`); of its five largest items with an amount, the first that passes the dollar check and for which `transcripts.excerpt_for_item` returns turns. The section shows the first turn's text in a `<blockquote>`, clipped to 60 words, then "<Speaker>, during discussion of <item headline linked> at the <Month D> meeting." with the speaker linked to their record when resolution attached a council member and plain when it attached a name alone, or "During discussion of <item> at the <Month D> meeting." when the turn's speaker is one of the two unnamed forms the transcript service produces ("Unknown speaker", "Speaker <n>"), then "Read the transcript →" to `/al/<slug>/meetings/<id>/transcript/#t-<seq>`. A cluster label such as `SPEAKER_04` never reaches the page. Without a public transcript, or with no item of that meeting that qualifies, the section is absent.

"Signals in the last 30 days" (`briefing.signals`, `briefing.SIGNAL_LABELS`): the process badges with a count above zero in the last 30 days (`query.badge_volume_recent`), in the order `hidden_on_consent` "Passed without discussion, on the consent agenda", `legal_settlement` "Legal settlements", `sole_source` "Contracts awarded without competing bids", `emergency_action` "Emergency actions", `amends_prior_contract` "Changes to earlier contracts", each one line "<label> — <n> items" linking to its category landing. A zero has no line; all zero, and the section is absent. The split-vote and contested badges are not listed; "Where they disagreed" is the page's one door for disagreement.

"Recent meetings" (`briefing.list_briefing_meetings`): the six newest non-hidden meetings, past or upcoming, that have at least one agenda item or at least one document link, each row the weekday date linked to the meeting, the title, "<n> items", the sum of the items' stored amounts when any item has one, "<k> split votes" when the meeting has chosen-source votes, followed by "from the video" when that source is the video, the document words (agenda · minutes · video) or "no documents linked"; an upcoming row says "upcoming" in place of its counts, and a cancelled row (`query.CANCELLED_PREDICATE_SQL`) says "cancelled". Under the list, "All meetings →" and one line "Follow: upcoming hearings (RSS) · city blog · coverage" linking `/al/<slug>/upcoming-hearings.rss`, `/al/<slug>/blog/feed.xml`, and `/coverage.rss`. A meeting with no items and no documents is excluded from the list; that is the page's defence against placeholder records, not their repair.

## The shared partials

`base.html` includes `partials/page_sources.html` and `partials/footer.html` on every page, and both read one Jinja global, `city_provenance(municipality)` (registered in `public.py`, backed by `briefing.city_provenance(municipality_id)`, cached per city for five minutes in `briefing._provenance_cache`): the last ingest time (`query.most_recent_ingest_at`), the lifetime meeting count, and the hostname of the newest non-hidden meeting's `source_url`. The freshness chip in the lead reads the same value, so the chip, the panel, and the footer cannot disagree. The source panel is headed "Where these records come from" (an eyebrow, not an `<h2>`) and says "The records on this page come from <host linked to that source_url>. docket.pub has indexed <n> <City> meetings; the last was added <Month D, YYYY>." with the host clause dropped when no `source_url` exists and the date clause when nothing has been ingested, then two links, "Source health" and "How we read minutes"; no adapter class, source path, schema, or search-index detail appears on a public page. The footer grid shows "Source" (the host, "· public domain") and "Updated" (the last ingest date) and is omitted without a municipality in context or without an ingest. Every page begins with `<a class="skip-link" href="#main">Skip to main content</a>`, visible on focus, and `<main id="main">`.

## The worker task

`briefing_facts` (`TASKS["briefing_facts"]` in `src/docket/worker/tasks.py`, `CronTrigger(hour=9, minute=45)` America/Chicago in `scheduler.py`, after the daily ingest, vote matching, and the process badges; Healthchecks variable `HEALTHCHECK_BRIEFING_FACTS_UUID`, check `docket-briefing-facts`) calls `briefing.recompute_all_flagged()`, which for every flagged city runs `briefing.recompute_city_facts(municipality_id)`: two SELECTs and one `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (municipality_id, fact_set) DO UPDATE` carrying both rows of `city_briefing_facts (municipality_id, fact_set, payload JSONB, computed_at)`. The `split_votes` payload is `{"window_days": 180, "since", "splits": [up to five, newest first], "last_split": the newest split ever or null, "votes_in_window"}`, each split `{"vote_id", "meeting_id", "meeting_date", "source", "result", "yeas", "nays", "abstentions", "item": {"id", "label"} or null, "linked_item_count", "all_consent" (every linked item's `is_consent`, false with no link), "dissenters": [{"member_name", "council_member_id" or null, "position"}]}`, the item set only when exactly one active link exists. The `member_counts` payload is `{"window_days": 365, "since", "members": {"<council_member_id>": {"roll_calls", "attended", "against"}}}` over chosen-source votes in non-hidden meetings of the window, where `attended` counts positions other than absent and `against` counts a no on a motion that passed or a yes on one that failed (`query.AGAINST_OUTCOME_PREDICATE`; an abstention and a vote on a tabled motion are roll calls attended, not votes against). `briefing.get_city_facts` reads both rows in one query; `briefing.facts_freshness` reports `warn` past 48 hours, which source health shows as stage "Briefing facts" with the `computed_at` time (the page itself renders from a stale row unchanged, since the chip reports ingest, not this table). `venv/bin/python -m docket.worker.scheduler --run-once briefing_facts` runs it by hand and logs `briefing_facts: recomputed <n> cities`; `docs/runbooks/cron-worker.md` holds the inventory row.

## Parameters on existing routes

The member page `/al/<slug>/council/<id>/` accepts `since=YYYY-MM-DD` (a malformed value is ignored): with it, `query.list_member_voting_history(..., since=)` restricts the history to meetings on or after that date under the one-source-per-meeting rule with `needs_review` votes excluded, the "All votes" and "Dissent" chips and "More →" carry the date, and the sentence "Showing votes since <Month D, YYYY>, one source per meeting." renders under the chips, and the Dissent filter applies that same `query.AGAINST_OUTCOME_PREDICATE`, so the council card's "voted against the outcome" number and the dated Dissent view it opens count the same votes. Without `since` the page behaves as before, its undated Dissent filter included (`D58` in `docs/DEBT.md`).

The search page `/search` with an empty query, a `city`, and `order=amount` lists that city's items of the last 90 days that have taken place and were not cancelled, excluding withdrawn items, and carry an amount (`query.list_items_by_amount`, at most 200 rows), keeps those that pass the dollar check, and pages them twenty at a time under the heading "Where the money went in <City>, last 90 days", so its first row is the money door's first line.

## Tests

`tests/support/briefing_seed.py` seeds the real Birmingham (the throwaway database already holds the city, its districts, its nine members, and its flag) with a past council meeting of four items, minutes votes (one split, one unanimous, one review-flagged video vote every count ignores), an upcoming meeting, a placeholder meeting, a public transcript, and two badges, every title prefixed `BRIEFING_TEST_`, with options (`votes`, including `tally_only` for a split with no roll call and `video` for a meeting whose chosen source is the video; `transcript`, including `low_speech` for a hidden transcript; `upcoming`, `dollars`, `badges`, `ghost`, `old_split`, `resolved_speaker`, `past_days` for a last meeting older than fourteen days, `undocumented` for a meeting with an item and no document links, and `cancelled` (`True` for a cancelled council meeting two days back with a posted consent item, `"upcoming"` for the same two days ahead)) for the partial, empty, and stale states the design's interaction table names, and `cleanup` by that prefix; `clear_caches` empties the overview and provenance caches between renders. `tests/web/test_city_briefing.py` covers each section's full, partial, and empty states and the opener's clause-dropping; `tests/web/test_city_briefing_a11y.py` renders the full page once and checks one h1, unique h2 texts, the eight headings above in order, no emoji in the `brf-` sections, every link with text, the meta description of at most 300 characters, sentences of at most 25 words, and none of the words `YTD`, `OCR`, `tsvector`, `CMS`, `Provenance`, `inferred`; `tests/services/test_briefing_facts.py` covers the task and the pure helpers. All run under `./tools/verify`.

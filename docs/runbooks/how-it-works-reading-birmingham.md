## The path and its rules

### Submitted (`submitted`)

- [ ] Description: A councilmember, the Mayor or the City Attorney submits the item, and it has to reach the City Clerk by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday.
- [ ] A councilmember, the Mayor or the City Attorney can submit an ordinance or resolution.
  - Quoted: "Ordinances, resolutions and other matters or subjects requiring action by the city council may be submitted by a councilmember, the mayor or city attorney."
  - General Code §2-2-15(c), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] Nothing is considered unless a councilmember sponsors it by moving its adoption.
  - Quoted: "Any councilmember may assume sponsorship thereof by moving that such ordinances, resolutions, matters or subjects to be adopted; otherwise, they shall not be considered."
  - General Code §2-2-15(c), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] Ordinances and resolutions are prepared by the City Attorney on the council president's written request, or reviewed by the City Attorney for form and legality.
  - Quoted: "Any ordinance or resolution shall be prepared by the city attorney upon the written request of the president of the city council. Any ordinance or resolution not prepared by the city attorney shall be submitted to him or her for review as to form and legality"
  - General Code §2-2-15(a), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] An item must reach the City Clerk by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday to be on the agenda.
  - Quoted: "All reports, communications, ordinances, resolutions, contract documents or other matters to be submitted to the city council shall be delivered to the city clerk by 5:00 p.m. Wednesday."
  - General Code §2-2-3(a), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] A late item needs unanimous consent, or an addendum approved by the council president at least 24 hours before the meeting.
  - Quoted: "shall not be voted on by council: (1) Except by unanimous consent of the city council; or (2) Unless approval has been obtained from the city council president for preparation of an addendum containing such matter at least 24 hours prior to the convening of the regularly scheduled council meeting."
  - General Code §2-2-3(a), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] Everything is sent to the Mayor for an administrative recommendation when it is introduced.
  - Quoted: "All ordinances, resolutions and contract documents shall, upon introduction, be submitted to the mayor for his or her administrative recommendation with reference thereto."
  - General Code §2-2-15(b), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances

### Committee (`committee`)

- [ ] Description: A committee of the council reviews the item and recommends it, or forwards it without a recommendation. A committee cannot decide it.
- [ ] Every ordinance or resolution goes to a committee before it goes to the council, unless the council waives that by unanimous consent.
  - Quoted: "Unless waived by unanimous consent, every ordinance or resolution requiring council approval shall be submitted to a committee of the council prior to submission of the item to the council."
  - General Code §2-2-15(c)(2), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] The committee reports at the next regular council meeting after it meets, unless the council grants more time; the council may take the item as reported, with the committee's amendments or substitutions.
  - Quoted: "The committee shall report thereon at the next regular meeting of the council after the committee meets, unless further time is granted by majority vote of the council. … the council may consider the ordinance or resolution as reported by the committee, including any amendments or substitutions proposed by the committee."
  - General Code §2-2-15(c)(2), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] When the council votes to refer an item to a committee, the motion must set a time of no more than 21 calendar days, or the next council meeting after the committee meets, whichever is less.
  - Quoted: "the motion to refer the matter to committee shall include a reasonable specified time not to exceed 21 calendar days or the next regularly scheduled council meeting following that committee meeting, whichever is less"
  - General Code §2-2-15(c)(5), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] A majority of the members voting, never fewer than four, can discharge a committee; the item then returns to the council for final action at the next regular meeting.
  - Quoted: "council, by the affirmative vote of a majority of the council members voting, but in no event less than four affirmative votes, may discharge the committee from further consideration of the matter referred, in which event the matter shall return to the council for final action at the next regularly scheduled meeting."
  - General Code §2-2-15(c)(5), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] A committee decides nothing. Only a vote of the full council at a regular meeting is final.
  - Quoted: "No action of any committee including the committee of the city council as a whole shall be considered final without a formal vote of the full council at a regular council meeting."
  - General Code §2-2-14(b), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] Committees are appointed by the council president, subject to the council's approval. Two members make a quorum. Whether the public may take part is at the chair's complete discretion.
  - Quoted: "All standing and special committees shall be appointed by the president of the city council, subject to approval by the city council. A quorum of two members of any committee except for the committee of the city council as a whole shall be required in order to conduct business. The chairperson of each committee shall exercise complete discretion in allowing public participation in committee meetings."
  - General Code §2-2-14(a), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] In practice a committee's minutes record one of a small set of outcomes. One is a recommendation to the council's agenda.
  - Quoted: "Item Recommended to the City Council Agenda."
  - Budget and Finance Committee minutes, 2026-10-12, `sources/committee-bf_min.txt`, https://www.birminghamal.gov/government/city-council/council/council-committees-2025-2027
- [ ] A committee can recommend items to the council's consent agenda.
  - Quoted: "Items Recommended to the City Council Consent Agenda."
  - Budget and Finance Committee minutes, 2026-10-12, `sources/committee-bf_min.txt`, https://www.birminghamal.gov/government/city-council/council/council-committees-2025-2027
- [ ] A committee can forward an item to the council without recommending it.
  - Quoted: "Item Recommended to the City Council Agenda with NO Recommendation."
  - Public Safety Committee minutes, 2026-10-13, `sources/committee-ps_min.txt`, https://www.birminghamal.gov/government/city-council/council/council-committees-2025-2027
- [ ] A committee can hear an item and take no action on it.
  - Quoted: "No Action – Information Only"
  - Committee of the Whole minutes, 2026-05-20, `sources/committee-cow_min.txt`, https://www.birminghamal.gov/government/city-council/council/council-committees-2025-2027
- [ ] The council's own handbook says committee members vote to recommend or not recommend an item, and that committees do not have the power to kill a bill.
  - Quoted: "kill a bill"
  - Citizen's Handbook 2024, p. 9, `sources/citizens-handbook-2024.txt`, https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/assets.birminghamal.gov/s3fs-public/2026-06/CITIZENS-HANDBOOK-2024.pdf
- [ ] A resident may ask to address a committee on a matter referred to it; the request goes through the City Clerk to the committee's chair.
  - Quoted: "Taxpayers or residents of the city or their authorized legal representatives shall be permitted to orally address any committee of the city council on any matter referred to that committee. Requests to appear before a committee of the city council shall be filed with the city clerk, who shall refer the same to the chairperson of the committee."
  - General Code §2-2-11(c), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] A rezoning has its own route. The Zoning Advisory Committee (the ZAC) of the Planning Commission, or the Commission itself, makes a recommendation; the council's Planning and Zoning Committee receives it; then the council holds a hearing.
  - Quoted: "The ZAC of the Commission or the Commission shall review and make recommendations to the Council on proposed amendments to this Ordinance and the official zoning map"

  - Quoted: "The proposed change may be deemed by the applicant to have been denied, if the Council takes no final action upon the same within 180 days after the filing of the application, or within 90 days after receipt by the Planning and Zoning Committee of the Council of the recommendation by the ZAC of the Commission or the Commission"

### On the agenda (`agenda`)

- [ ] Description: The item is printed on a council meeting's agenda, under the consent agenda, final passage, or introduction and first reading.
- [ ] The council takes its business in a fixed order: roll call; minutes; communications from the Mayor; boards and agencies; the consent agenda; final passage; introduction and first reading; old and new business; requests from the public; presentations; adjournment.
  - Quoted: "(5) Consideration of the consent agenda. (6) Consideration of ordinances and resolutions for final passage. (7) Introduction and first reading of ordinances and resolutions."
  - General Code §2-2-7, `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] A person speaking on an agenda item that is not a public hearing has three minutes, following the council's presentations.
  - Quoted: "All persons addressing specific agenda items that are not designated public hearings shall be granted three minutes to speak following city council presentations"
  - General Code §2-2-7(9)a, `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances
- [ ] For general public comment, a speaker signs the list before the meeting and has three minutes; all such comment is limited to thirty minutes in total, and nobody may speak at two consecutive meetings.
  - Quoted: "Such person shall be allowed to speak for three minutes provided that the total time for all such oral presentations shall not exceed 30 minutes unless further time is granted by the city council. No person shall be allowed to address the city council at two consecutive meetings unless granted by the city council."
  - General Code §2-2-11(a), `sources/general-code-title-2-chapter-2.txt`, https://library.municode.com/al/birmingham/codes/code_of_ordinances

## Open questions

- [ ] The committee rule and what the agenda shows: The code says every item goes to a committee unless that is waived by unanimous consent. Many agenda items show only a recommendation by a department director and name no committee. Whether those were waived, or whether the code's reference to the committee of the whole in §2-2-15(c)(4) covers them, is not shown in any record we have read.
- [ ] Four votes or five for a permanent ordinance: Both Birmingham-specific sources, the General Code and the Mayor-Council Act, say four votes. Alabama's general law, §11-45-2(b), says an ordinance of permanent operation needs a majority of the members elected, which on a council of nine is five. We quote the Birmingham rule and note the general-law wording; we do not rule on the conflict.
- [ ] The ** marker on agenda items: Some agenda items carry a ** marker. It is not defined in anything we have read, so this page does not explain it.
- [ ] Who decides which items go on the consent agenda: Budget and Finance Committee minutes show the committee recommending items to the consent agenda. No rule we have read says who makes the choice.
- [ ] Why liquor-licence and inoperable-vehicle items have hearings: They are marked (ph) on every agenda. The code sections that require those hearings sit in other titles of the General Code, which we have not read.
- [ ] The current list of committees: Resolution 1958-25, adopted 2025-11-04, names six committees and the Committee of the Whole. The city's website now lists eight. We have not found the date of the change or the instrument that made it.
- [ ] Electronic attendance and §2-2-19: An ordinance on the 2026-06-16 agenda proposed adding electronic attendance to the code at §2-2-19. The copy of the code we saved shows that section as reserved.

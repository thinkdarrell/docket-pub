III.   ZONING CASES
       ZAC2026-08010 - The City is proposing an amendment to the Zoning Ordinance to remove
       Title 2 and Title 3. The final two rezoning public hearings—Pratt–Ensley on September 22 and
       the Southern Area on October 20—will complete the citywide rezoning effort. Once these
       hearings are concluded, the entire city will be under the updated zoning framework, and Title
       2, which contains the former zoning classifications, will no longer be necessary. In addition,
       Title 3 is proposed for removal because its supplementary information has already been
       incorporated into Title 1, rendering it redundant.
       The Planning Commission voted to recommend approval of the request.
       Action Taken:
       Councilor O’Quinn Motioned to Approve.
       Councilor Tate Seconded the Motion.
       Items Recommended to the City Council Agenda.

       ZAC2026-08012 - Text Amendment to the City’s Zoning Ordinance: The City is
       proposing to amend Title 1, Chapter 3, Article 1, Section 8 (Affordable Housing Density
       Bonus), Table 1.03.104 per the following: 1. Remove D-5 from the table.
       The Planning Commission voted to recommend approval of the request.
       Action Taken:
       Councilor O’Quinn Motioned to Approve.
       Councilor Tate Seconded the Motion.
       Items Recommended to the City Council Agenda.

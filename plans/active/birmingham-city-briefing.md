# Rebuild the Birmingham city page as a briefing: a source-linked opener behind a per-city flag, doors for money, disagreement, council, topics, and transcript, one meetings list, plain-language signals, and a worker task that precomputes the vote and member facts

This ExecPlan is a living document. The sections `Progress`, `Surprises & Discoveries`, `Decision Log`, and `Outcomes & Retrospective` must be kept up to date as work proceeds.

This document must be maintained in accordance with `plans/PLANS.md`.

## Purpose / Big Picture

docket.pub publishes Alabama council records with every fact linked to the city's own document. Its Birmingham page at `https://docket.pub/al/birmingham/` is today an inventory: a name and freshness chip, three large numbers, an empty "No agenda posted yet" card, two strips of identical "Regular City Council Meeting" cards, twenty-two tile and chip doors, a grid of dollar items, a meetings table that repeats the strip, and a source panel written in developer vocabulary (adapter class, source path, `tsvector`). The first sentence with meaning is five screens down. The approved design, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-birmingham-city-page-design.md`, turns the page into a briefing.

After this plan, a reader who opens `/al/birmingham/` (locally: `venv/bin/flask run`, then GET `/al/birmingham/`) sees, in this order: the existing lead (eyebrow, h1, freshness chip); an opener of two short prose beats filled from counted rows, such as "Last meeting. The council met on Tuesday, October 14 and acted on 63 items. The largest was a $10.1 million contract for citywide road resurfacing. One vote split: the KultureCity campus item passed 6 to 1, with Hunter Williams voting no. Read the October 14 meeting →" and "Next meeting. Tuesday, October 21, with 42 items on the agenda. See the agenda →", every clause a link to the meeting, item, or member page it came from, any clause the rows cannot fill dropped rather than defaulted, and the opener's text reused as the page's meta description; one search field scoped to Birmingham; then doors headed "Where the money went", "Where they disagreed", "The council", "What they keep dealing with", "Said at the meeting", and "Signals in the last 30 days"; and one "Recent meetings" list of six rows followed by one line of three feed links. The KPI strip, the "On the agenda" and "Last week" strips, the priority tile grids, the eleven topic chips, the tier legend, the "Notable items" grid, and the duplicate meetings table are gone. Each section renders only when its data exists, so `/al/mobile/`, `/al/homewood/`, and `/al/vestavia_hills/` still return 200 with the lead, the search field, and whatever lists their rows support, and no opener prose, because the opener runs only where the municipality's metadata carries `"briefing": true`, which a migration sets for Birmingham alone. The site-wide source panel on every page names the city website the records come from, the indexed meeting count, the last-indexed date, and links to source health and the methodology page, with the developer rows and the duplicate h2 removed; the footer's "Updated" date is the municipality's last-ingest date, the same value the freshness chip shows; `base.html` gains a skip link. On the worker, `venv/bin/python -m docket.worker.scheduler --run-once briefing_facts` recomputes, for every flagged city, the split votes, the unanimity fallback, and the per-member twelve-month attendance and against-the-outcome counts into one new table, read by the city route in one query. `./tools/verify` passes with web-tier tests per section for its empty, partial, and full states against the throwaway database plus one for the opener's clause-dropping. A new present-tense spec `docs/specs/city-page.md` describes the page and the task.

The design's own anti-goals bind every milestone: no sentence whose number, name, or date lacks a link to its source; no clause filled with a default; no door onto an empty page or a zero; no cold load slower than today's (the five-minute overview cache stays and anything heavier than today's queries is precomputed); no vote fact from video and from minutes merged without its source label.

## Decision Log

- Decision: the opener's facts are computed per request from a handful of single-meeting queries, and only the heavy facts (the split votes of the last 180 days, the unanimity fallback, the per-member twelve-month counts) come from the precomputed table.
  Rationale: the design leaves this to the builder. The next-meeting clause depends on the clock (a meeting stops being "upcoming" three hours after its start), so a once-a-day row would name a meeting that already happened for most of a day; the last-meeting clause touches one meeting's items and votes, which are cheap and indexed; and the page is cached for five minutes anyway. The heavy queries are the ones the route switched off for cold-load cost (`contested = []`, `recent_votes = []` in `src/docket/web/public.py`), and a per-member twelve-month aggregate over all votes is heavier still.
  Date/Author: 2026-10-19, plan author (Claude, cycle-plan).
- Decision: the precompute table is `city_briefing_facts (municipality_id, fact_set, payload JSONB, computed_at)` with primary key `(municipality_id, fact_set)` and two fact sets, `split_votes` and `member_counts`; a city is rebuilt by one `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (municipality_id, fact_set) DO UPDATE` statement carrying both rows. The worker task is named `briefing_facts`, scheduled at 09:45 America/Chicago, with the Healthchecks variable `HEALTHCHECK_BRIEFING_FACTS_UUID` and the check name `docket-briefing-facts`.
  Rationale: one row per city per fact set is what the design asks for, and a single upsert statement is "rebuilt in place per city with one statement; never swept row by row" (`docs/PRINCIPLES.md`, Production is not a sandbox). 09:45 is after the daily ingest (06:00), after `vote_matching` (09:00), which links votes to items and so decides what a split vote names, and after `process_badges` (09:30); earlier than that the row would be computed from yesterday's links. The names follow the existing pattern in `src/docket/worker/health.py` and `docs/runbooks/cron-worker.md`.
  Date/Author: 2026-10-19, plan author.
- Decision: the source-per-meeting rule is one SQL fragment owned by `src/docket/services/briefing.py`: for each meeting, the chosen source is `minutes_text` when the meeting has any vote with that source and `needs_review = FALSE`, otherwise the alphabetically first other source among its non-review votes; every count on the page (the opener's split or unanimous sentence, the disagreement door, the per-member numbers, the split count in the meetings list, and the member page when its `since` parameter is given) uses that fragment and excludes `needs_review` votes.
  Rationale: the design says minutes when present, otherwise video, and forbids adjudicating. `votes.source` may also hold `api` or `manual` (migration 001), which Birmingham's data is not expected to contain; "alphabetically first other source" makes the rule total without inventing a preference order the design did not state, and the operator's pre-merge count (Concrete Steps) shows whether any other value exists.
  Date/Author: 2026-10-19, plan author.
- Decision: the dollar check is `passes_dollar_check(title, description, headline, dollars_amount)` in `src/docket/services/briefing.py`: it passes when `extract_dollar_amounts(title + " " + description)` from `src/docket/analysis/structured_facts.py` returns exactly one amount, or when some amount `h` found by the same function in the headline satisfies `abs(h - dollars_amount) < p / 2`, where `p` is the largest power of ten that divides `h` (so `$10.1 million` has `p = 100000` and matches a stored 10,125,000; `$7M` has `p = 1000000` and does not match a stored 39,500,000).
  Rationale: the design states both conditions in words and leaves "within rounding to the headline's precision" to the builder; the power-of-ten rule is the mechanical reading that gives the KultureCity example the answer the design expects.
  Date/Author: 2026-10-19, plan author.
- Decision: in the opener, exactly one dissenter is named and linked to their member record; when a split has two or more dissenters, the clause says "with two members voting no" (or "abstaining") and that phrase links to the vote's anchor on the meeting page; a dissenter whose `council_member_id` is NULL is treated as unnamed and the phrase links to the vote anchor. The split item itself is named but not linked in the opener; it is linked in the disagreement door.
  Rationale: the design caps each beat at three links (item, member, action) and forbids two adjacent links; naming two linked members would make four. An unresolved name cannot link to a record, and "source or silence" forbids a name with no link, so the meeting page's roll call is the source the phrase points at.

## Context and Orientation

### The application in one paragraph

docket.pub is a Python 3.10 Flask application with one PostgreSQL database. Public pages live in `src/docket/web/public.py` (routes) and `src/docket/web/templates/` (Jinja templates; `base.html` wraps every page and includes `partials/masthead.html`, the page's `content` block, `partials/page_sources.html`, and `partials/footer.html`). Routes call functions in `src/docket/services/` (mostly `query.py`), and services own every database transaction through `db_cursor()` from `src/docket/db.py`; `ARCHITECTURE.md` forbids SQL in `src/docket/web/`. A cron worker, `src/docket/worker/scheduler.py`, registers jobs from the `TASKS` dict in `src/docket/worker/tasks.py`; each task runs through `_safe_run`, which pings Healthchecks.io via `src/docket/worker/health.py` using the environment variable named in `TASK_UUID_ENV`. Schema changes are numbered modules under `src/docket/migrations/` listed in `MIGRATIONS` in `runner.py`; the last is `036_discrepancy_anchor_and_minutes_check`, so this plan's migration is `037`. Styles are plain CSS files under `src/docket/web/static/` (`layout.css` holds most page sections including the council card at lines 388 to 445 and the source panel at line 1053; `councilmatic.css` holds the strips, topic chips, and legislation cards; `mobile.css` loads last and holds the phone-width rules; `tweaks.css` holds the footer grid). There is no build step and no JavaScript library beyond HTMX.

### Terms used in this plan

An "agenda item" is a row of `agenda_items` (title, description, `headline` written by the AI pipeline, `dollars_amount` extracted from the text, `is_consent`, `topic`, `item_number`). A "vote" is a row of `votes` (result, yeas, nays, abstentions, `source` in `video_ocr`, `minutes_text`, `api`, `manual`; `needs_review`), attached to agenda items zero-to-many through `vote_agenda_items` (`is_active`, `provisional`), and broken down per member in `member_votes` (`council_member_id`, nullable; `member_name`; `position` in `yea`, `yes`, `nay`, `no`, `abstain`, `absent`). A "council member" is a row of `council_members` joined to `districts` (`number`). A "meeting" is a row of `meetings` (`meeting_type` in `council`, `work_session`, `bza`, `planning`, `special`; `meeting_date`; `start_time`; `agenda_url`, `minutes_url`, `video_url`, `source_url`; `created_at`, which the freshness chip treats as the ingest time; `is_hidden`). "Upcoming" means the predicate in `src/docket/services/meeting_time.py`: `meeting_date + COALESCE(start_time, 12:00) + 3 hours` is still in the future in America/Chicago; `query.py` aliases it to `mt.` columns as `_UPCOMING_PREDICATE_MT`. A "transcript" is public when `transcripts.uploaded_at IS NOT NULL AND status <> 'low_speech'` (`public_predicate` in `src/docket/services/transcripts.py`); `excerpt_for_item(item_id)` returns the first turns tagged with an item, each turn carrying `anchor` (`t-<seq>`), `speaker_label`, `speaker_member_id`, and `texts`. A "badge" is a row of `agenda_item_badges` with a slug from `priority_badge_templates`; the five process badges this page shows are `hidden_on_consent`, `legal_settlement`, `sole_source`, `emergency_action`, `amends_prior_contract`, and the four Birmingham policy badges (`list_city_policy_badges`) are named Blight Accountability, Housing Stability, Property Recovery, and Public-Safety Tech & Privacy. A "category landing" is `/al/<slug>/<badge_slug>/`. Topic slugs come from `src/docket/enrichment/topics.py`; the one to exclude is `routine` ("Routine & Procedural"). The municipality's `metadata` is a JSONB column added by migration 029 holding `council_type`, `county`, `population`.

    -- 1. vote sources Birmingham holds; the design assumes only minutes_text and video_ocr
    SELECT v.source, v.needs_review, count(*) FROM votes v JOIN meetings m ON m.id = v.meeting_id
     WHERE m.municipality_id = (SELECT id FROM municipalities WHERE slug = 'birmingham') GROUP BY 1, 2 ORDER BY 1, 2;
    -- 2. meetings with votes from more than one source; the design expects few
    SELECT count(*) FROM (SELECT meeting_id FROM votes GROUP BY meeting_id HAVING count(DISTINCT source) > 1) s;
    -- 3. council meetings of the last 90 days with no items (the ghosts the list filters); the design expects some
    SELECT count(*) FILTER (WHERE NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM agenda_items a WHERE a.meeting_id = m.id)) AS no_items, count(*) AS total
      FROM meetings m WHERE m.municipality_id = (SELECT id FROM municipalities WHERE slug = 'birmingham')
       AND m.meeting_type = 'council' AND m.is_hidden = FALSE AND m.meeting_date >= current_date - 90;
    -- 4. member votes of the last 12 months whose member is unresolved; the design assumes a small share
    SELECT count(*) FILTER (WHERE mv.council_member_id IS NULL) AS unresolved, count(*) AS total
      FROM member_votes mv JOIN votes v ON v.id = mv.vote_id JOIN meetings m ON m.id = v.meeting_id
     WHERE m.municipality_id = (SELECT id FROM municipalities WHERE slug = 'birmingham') AND m.meeting_date >= current_date - 365;
    -- 5. proxy for the dollar check: items of the last 90 days with an amount, and how many have exactly one '$' in their text
    SELECT count(*) AS with_amount,
           count(*) FILTER (WHERE length(coalesce(ai.title,'') || coalesce(ai.description,'')) - length(replace(coalesce(ai.title,'') || coalesce(ai.description,''), '$', '')) = 1) AS one_dollar_sign
      FROM agenda_items ai JOIN meetings m ON m.id = ai.meeting_id
     WHERE m.municipality_id = (SELECT id FROM municipalities WHERE slug = 'birmingham') AND m.meeting_date >= current_date - 90 AND ai.dollars_amount IS NOT NULL;
    -- 6. D27: the money query's plan; a Seq Scan on agenda_items here is the index trigger
    EXPLAIN SELECT ai.id, ai.dollars_amount FROM agenda_items ai JOIN meetings mt ON ai.meeting_id = mt.id
     WHERE mt.municipality_id = (SELECT id FROM municipalities WHERE slug = 'birmingham') AND mt.is_hidden = FALSE
       AND mt.meeting_date >= current_date - 90 AND ai.dollars_amount IS NOT NULL ORDER BY ai.dollars_amount DESC LIMIT 40;
    -- 7. public transcripts, for the transcript door
    SELECT count(*) FROM transcripts t JOIN meetings m ON m.id = t.meeting_id
     WHERE m.municipality_id = (SELECT id FROM municipalities WHERE slug = 'birmingham') AND t.uploaded_at IS NOT NULL AND t.status <> 'low_speech';

                                RESOLUTION NO. 1951-25

      WHEREAS, it is the proud privilege of this Legislative Body to recognize individuals who
                                            NOV 4 2025                                           3

have gone far beyond the call of duty in serving and securing the best interests of the Birmingham
City Council. With this aim in mind, the City Council pauses today to honor one of its own—the
esteemed Ms. Cheryl Anderson Kidd, JD, MBA, who is celebrating her retirement on Tuesday,
November 4, 2025, after 23 years and 6 months of dedicated service as Council Administrator to
the Birmingham City Council; and

       WHEREAS, over the course of her illustrious career, Ms. Kidd has worked with six
Mayors, including two interims, nine council presidents, numerous councilmembers, and many
other elected officials to ensure efficient legislation and stable budgetary methods to solidify and
continue the City’s progress and prosperity; and

       WHEREAS, Ms. Kidd endeavored to create a workplace within the City Council’s Office
that would elevate opportunities for those who desire to serve. With approval, Ms. Kidd created
the position of Deputy Administrator, and since its inception, four deputies have served in that
capacity. This was the start of ensuring continuity of information and a broader base for
management and development of staff; and

      WHEREAS, office operations and staff development were near and dear to the heart of
Ms. Kidd. Committee Roundup was instituted to align staff communication with the council
committee's priorities. Ms. Kidd encouraged and ensured funding availability for staff
development; and

      WHEREAS, Ms. Kidd insisted that every council request was a priority to be fulfilled with
competency and courtesy. Equitable distribution of services was a mandate. Council branding and
engagement were brought to the forefront of each staff work session. Public Information was
expanded to on-board excellent talent in videography, website management, and external
engagement; and

       WHEREAS, Ms. Kidd fervently believes that God does not always call the equipped, but
he does equip those he has called. Believing that this journey was a calling, she is quick to give all
the glory to God for his providential protection; and

        WHEREAS, it is befitting to say as recorded in John 3:21 (NRSV) “But those who do what
is true come to the light, so that it may be clearly seen that their deeds have been done in God”;
and

       WHEREAS, Ms. Kidd is the loving mother of two daughters and the doting grandmother
of one granddaughter and one grandson. She is an active member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority,
Incorporated, and the Birmingham Chapter of the Links, Incorporated.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Council of the City of Birmingham,
with Mayor Randall L. Woodfin concurring, pauses in its deliberations to join family and friends
in celebrating Ms. Cheryl Anderson Kidd’s well-deserved retirement. We extend a Big THANK
YOU to her and wish her all the best as she embarks upon her next chapter.

       WHEREAS, the federal government shut down on October 1, 2025, has affected the

the Alabama Department of Human Resources (DHR) received specific guidance from the U.S.
Department of Agriculture Food and Nutrition Service (FSN) that effective November 1, 2025,

to the elderly citizens, and poor and needy citizens residing within the City, in the month of
November 2025 during the federal government shutdown. The Mayor shall be authorized to enter
such contracts as he determines to be necessary to carry out the intent of this ordinance, provided
such contracts comply with Alabama purchasing laws and other provisions of State and Federal
law, as well as the ordinances of the City of Birmingham. Until the above referenced appropriation
has been expended, any resident of the City of Birmingham who provides evidence that they are
eligible to receive Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits, shall be eligible
to receive supplemental nutrition assistance provided by the City of Birmingham during the month
of November 2025, while the federal government shutdown continues. Evidence of eligibility shall
include proof of residence and a presentation of at least one of the following: (1) a valid Electronic
Benefits Transfer (EBT) card; (2) a county SNAP identification card; (3) a SNAP verification form
printed from the MyDHR Client Portal; or (4) documentation from DHR confirming eligibility for
SNAP benefits. A single person who is eligible for these supplemental benefits shall receive a one-
time allotment of up to $100.00 and an eligible family shall receive a one-time allotment of up to
$200.00.

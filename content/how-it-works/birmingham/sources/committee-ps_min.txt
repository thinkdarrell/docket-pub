     enforcement purposes, with Watch Systems, LLC for a period not to exceed three (3) years
     beginning on October 24, 2026, and ending October 23, 2029. Watch Systems, LLC will
     provide to the City a subscription to OffenderWatch® mobile application and software services,
     including but not limited to; providing user training, Help Desk support, software maintenance,
     continuous software upgrades as they are made available, and also providing OffenderWatch
     Post Card Notification service which sends postal notification to a specified area surrounding
     the offender.

STATEMENT OF CONDUCT OF BUSINESS
All items designated as “Consent” are routine and non-controversial and will be
approved by one motion. All items on the “Consent Agenda” will be announced by
reading the Item Numbers only. No separate discussion of these items will be permitted
unless a Councilmember or the Mayor requests the item be removed from the “Consent
Agenda”. Additionally, a Citizen interested in addressing the Council with respect to an
item on the “Consent Agenda” designated for “Public Hearing” (“ph”) may request the
item be removed from the “Consent Agenda” to hold the hearing on the item. Any
item(s) removed from the “Consent Agenda” will revert to its normal place on the
Agenda Order of Business.

For remaining matters, all matters of permanent operation (“P”) will be read, and all
Public Hearings (“ph”) will be announced.
